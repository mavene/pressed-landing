Get in touch

Write to support@paradimn.xyz and tell us what happened. It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it went wrong. We read everything.

How to save a link

  1. In Safari, or any app with a Share button, tap Share.
  2. Look for Pressed in the row of apps. The first time, scroll to the end of the row, tap More, and switch Pressed on.
  3. Tap Pressed. The link lands on The Pile.

You can also open The Pile when it's empty and tap How to save a link for the same steps.

Common questions

I can't find Pressed in the Share sheet.

Scroll to the end of the row of apps, tap More, and switch Pressed on. If it still isn't there, open Pressed once, then try again.

My link has no title or picture.

Some websites don't let apps look at their pages, or are slow to answer. Pressed shows the site's name instead and tries again a few times over the next day or so. You can also type your own title when you press the link.

How long does a link last?

Three, seven or fourteen days, whichever you choose in Settings (seven is the default). A link lasts through the whole of its last day, until 23:59:59. Changing the setting applies to links you save afterwards.

A link crumbled. Can I get it back?

Save it again and it starts afresh. If you open an expired link from the home-screen widget, it gets one last day, once.

How does Tonight's stack choose cards?

It picks about three cards by due date, how long you say you have (5, 15 or 30 minutes, or any) and a little variety, so tonight's reading is short enough to finish.

I'm not getting the evening reminder.

Check that reminders are on in Pressed's settings, that notifications are allowed for Pressed in iOS Settings, and that you have at least one link waiting. Pressed doesn't send a reminder when your pile is empty.

Does it sync between my devices?

Not yet. Your links live on the iPhone you saved them on.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

Everything stays on your iPhone. You can delete a bookmark from inside the app, and deleting Pressed removes everything it stored. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.

Which iPhones does it work on?

iPhones running iOS 18 or later.
