Privacy Policy

Your reading list is your business.

Last updated 13 November 2026

The short version

Pressed doesn't collect your data. What you save stays on your iPhone: no account, no Pressed server, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

Who we are

Pressed is made by Dian Maisara under Paradimn. You can reach us at support@paradimn.xyz.

What's stored on your iPhone

When you save a link, Pressed keeps this on your device only:

  • the link, its page title, site name, a small preview picture and an estimated reading or listening time;
  • when you saved it, when it is due, and whether you pressed it or it crumbled;
  • the optional title and note you type when you press a link;
  • your settings: how long a link lasts (3, 7 or 14 days), and whether you want an evening reminder, and when.

Only Pressed, its Share extension and its widget can read it. We never receive any of it. Links that crumble stay on your device as a record, so Pressed can count them, until you delete the app. Like other apps' data, it may be in the iPhone backups you make yourself.

Three more things use that same data, all on your device:

  • Reminders. If you allow notifications, Pressed schedules them on your iPhone, with no push server. Turn them off in Pressed's settings or iOS Settings.
  • Background updates. iOS may wake Pressed briefly to import shared links and refresh reminders.
  • The widget. It shows titles and due dates to anyone who can see your home screen.

When you save or open a link

Saving. To find a title, picture and reading time, Pressed asks the website directly from your iPhone, using Apple's own link and networking tools. That site, and the server holding its preview picture, see an ordinary web request, including your IP address and device type, and their own privacy policies apply. This is the only time Pressed itself uses the internet.

Opening. Links open in Safari's in-app reader. Pressed can't see what you do there, and Safari's protections apply.

What we don't do

  • Ask you to make an account.
  • Use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising tools or any third-party software that collects data.
  • Track you across other companies' apps or websites, or use your device's advertising identifier.
  • Sell, rent or share your data.

If you've chosen to share iPhone analytics with app developers, Apple may pass us anonymous crash and usage reports. We can't tell who they come from.

Your choices

You can delete a pressed bookmark inside the app, and deleting Pressed from your iPhone removes everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. If a future version of Pressed needs to handle your information differently, we'll tell you in the app first.
